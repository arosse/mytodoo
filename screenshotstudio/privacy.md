# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 11th, 2026*

**Screenshot Studio** is developed by **GreenAnt Apps**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information related to the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Screenshot Studio does not collect, store, or share any personal information.** The screenshots you import, the texts you write, and the projects you create are stored exclusively on your device and are never transmitted to us or any third party.

## 2. Third-Party Services

Screenshot Studio does not use any third-party services and does not connect to the internet.

## 3. Ads and Analytics

Screenshot Studio contains **no advertisements** and collects **no analytics data** of any kind.

## 4. In-App Purchases

There are **no in-app purchases** or monetization features in Screenshot Studio.

## 5. Your Screenshots, Exports and Sharing

The app lets you import screenshots from your device using the Android photo picker. **The app can only see the images you choose to select**, and they are processed entirely on your device. They are never uploaded, transmitted, or made available to us or to any third party.

You can save your finished images to your device's gallery, or send them with your device's "Share" function. When you share an image, you decide which app receives it and who you send it to. That transfer happens between you and the app you choose, and we never receive it. Once you share an image with another app, that app's own privacy policy applies.

## 6. Data Security

Your projects and images are stored locally in the app's own storage on your device. We have no access to them. Uninstalling the app deletes them. Images you have saved to your gallery or shared with other apps are not affected, since they are your files.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Screenshot Studio is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the updated version will be published on our website and reflected in the app.
