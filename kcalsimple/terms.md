## 3. AI Estimates

KcalSimple can estimate calories from a photo of a meal or from a description of a physical activity, using AI (Artificial Intelligence) provided by Google's Gemini. These features are optional and can be turned off in Settings. A limited number of AI estimates can be used for free each day; the Premium subscription (see Section 4) increases this limit.

AI estimates are produced automatically and are approximate. They may be inaccurate and are not medical or nutritional advice; always check and adjust the values before logging them. AI estimates need an internet connection and may occasionally be unavailable, paused or limited. Please submit only photos of food and descriptions of activities, and do not attempt to misuse or automate the service.
