KcalSimple

Simple calorie and weight tracking, without complex macros.

Health & fitness Android
Get it on Google Play Privacy policy
Overview

Calorie tracking without the clutter.

KcalSimple is a simple and easy calorie and weight tracker for Android. Log what you eat in seconds and keep an eye on your daily calories and your weight progress. There are no complex macros, and no accounts or sign-up questionnaires. It's free and without ads, and comes with AI calorie estimation functions for maximum convenience. It's also privacy friendly: your diary and weights stay on your device, with no tracking or analytics.

Features

Minimal on purpose.

Minimal by design

No complex macros, no distractions. Just track your calories and your weight.

No account needed

No accounts and no sign-up questionnaires. Open the app and start logging.

Free, no ads

Free to use and without ads. Optional Premium for more AI estimates.

Privacy friendly

Your food logs and weights stay on your device. No tracking, no analytics.

AI calorie estimates

Snap a photo of your meal or describe your workout to get a quick and easy calorie estimate.

Backup & restore

Export your data to a file and restore it anytime. Your backups stay with you.
