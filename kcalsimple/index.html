KcalSimple

Simple calorie and weight tracking, without complex macros.

Health & fitness Android
Get it on Google Play Privacy policy
Overview

Calorie tracking without the clutter.

KcalSimple is a minimal calorie and weight tracker for Android. Log what you eat in seconds and keep an eye on your daily calories and your weight progress. There are no complex macros or fancy AI features, and no accounts or sign-up questionnaires. It's completely free and without ads. It's also privacy friendly: everything you log stays on your device, with no tracking or analytics.

Features

Minimal on purpose.

Minimal by design

No complex macros, no fancy AI features. Just track your calories and your weight.

No account needed

No accounts and no sign-up questionnaires. Open the app and start logging.

Completely free

Free to use and without ads.

Privacy friendly

Your food logs stay on your device. No tracking, no analytics, no personal data collected.
