Formula 10

Can you make 10? A math puzzle game.

Puzzle game Android
Get it on Google Play Privacy policy
Overview

Turn the numbers into 10.

Formula 10 is a math puzzle game with over 9,000 puzzles across three difficulty levels. Each puzzle gives you four digits: combine them with +, −, ×, ÷, √ and ^ to make exactly 10.

It's free, works offline and has no ads.
