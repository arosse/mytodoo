# Privacy Policy

*Last updated: October 3rd, 2026*

**App Earnings Tracker** is developed by **GreenAnt Apps**.

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle any information related to the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

**App Earnings Tracker does not collect, store, or share any personal information.** All data you enter in the app (sales entries, imported earnings reports, products, notes, settings) is stored exclusively on your device and never transmitted to us or any third party.

## 2. Third-Party Services

App Earnings Tracker does not use any third-party services and does not connect to the internet.

## 3. Ads and Analytics

App Earnings Tracker contains **no advertisements** and collects **no analytics data** of any kind.

## 4. In-App Purchases

There are **no in-app purchases** or monetization features in App Earnings Tracker.

## 5. Data Export and Import

The app lets you import your app sales data (such as the Earnings Reports you download from the Google Play Console) from a file on your device, and export your data to a CSV file.

We understand that earnings reports contain sensitive financial information. **Imported files and the data extracted from them are processed entirely on your device.** They are never uploaded, transmitted, shared, or made available to us or to any third party. Exported files are saved only where you choose on your device, and we never receive them.

## 6. Data Security

All data entered in the app is stored locally on your device using a standard SQLite database. We have no access to this data. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## 7. Children's Privacy

App Earnings Tracker is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, the updated version will be published on our website and reflected in the app.
