Overview

Find testers by testing for others.

Google Play asks new personal developer accounts to run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days before an app can be published to production. Finding those testers is hard when you are building on your own.

TestCircle brings indie developers together to solve it. You join a testing group that tests in sync: everyone installs each other's apps and works through the same phases together. Throughout the cycle, everyone shares structured feedback, so you finish with real testers and useful insight into your app.

Single Tests complement your group and can boost your app's visibility: test other developers' apps to earn credits, then spend them to post your own app and get feedback from additional users beyond your testing group.

How it works

From sign-up to feedback summary.

Submit your app

Install TestCircle, sign in with your Google account and submit your app to a testing group.

Set up Google Group access

Join the TestCircle Google Group, then add it as a tester on your app's closed testing track. The app guides you through both.

Test and give feedback

Everyone in your group installs each other's apps. Over five testing phases across 14 days, members test each app and share structured feedback.

Get your results

At the end of the cycle, receive an AI-generated summary of the feedback on your app and export a detailed report as PDF or CSV.

Features

Everything you need for closed testing.

Testing groups

Get matched with other indie developers who all install and test each other's apps. Groups aim for 16 testers, leaving a margin above the 12 you need in case a few drop out mid-cycle.

Structured feedback

Guided feedback forms and quick tests across the cycle ensure regular app usage throughout the closed testing and keep feedback consistent and useful.

Free to use

No ads, no subscriptions and no sign-up fees. Synchronized 14-day group testing is completely free.

Single Tests & credits

Complement your group with Single Tests. Test other developers' apps to earn credits, then spend them to post your own app and get feedback from additional users beyond your testing group.

In-app messaging

Talk directly with the other developers in your testing group, using text and screenshots. Direct communication makes it more efficient to clarify feedback and debug issues.

Summaries & exports

Export a detailed report with all your testing feedback and the AI-generated summary, as a PDF or CSV.
